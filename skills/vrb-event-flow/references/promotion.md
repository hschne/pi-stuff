## Content Rules

- Preserve confirmed names, titles, dates, and venue facts exactly.
- Write direct promotional copy without engagement bait or invented claims.
- X uses one post, not a thread.
- X links to Meetup only.
- Bluesky and LinkedIn link to Meetup and Luma.
- Speaker spotlights use the corresponding talk cover.
- Announcement, reminder, and sponsor/venue posts use the landscape event cover.
- Alt text describes the meaningful text and person shown without repeating decorative details.
- The approved Markdown is the exact scheduling source. Update it before requesting final Buffer approval.
